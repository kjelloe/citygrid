# 10 - QA

City Grid already has the right shape: node tests for everything pure, a Playwright gate per
slice that drives the real page, and a rule that the fail count is what is read. The engine
adds gates in the fable51 pattern, where every one was built because a class of bug had no
other way of being seen.

## 10.1 Node tests, for the model

`client/world/` is pure, so every derivation is a fixture assertion:

- corridors: a straight road of N tiles is one corridor of `N × TILE_M`; a T is one node and
  three corridors; a bend is two corridors and a connector.
- lots: every building has a frontage; the frontage faces a road tile when one exists; a 2×1
  building has two bays.
- lane graph: every link has a successor except an exit; no link is shorter than a car; every
  node with degree ≥ 3 has a signal phase.
- heights: a road corridor is level across its width; a building's seat is the minimum of its
  corners; `surfaceAt` on a road tile centre says `road`.
- parameters: the L2 and L3 parameter sets for one building id agree on variant, roof hue and
  wall colour (the thing that would otherwise drift silently).

## 10.2 Capture

`tools/play_shot.mjs` screenshots the real page. The engine's version takes a **viewpoint
list** in the fable51 form - position, heading, pitch, fov, mode, time, tier - in a JSON file,
so the same twelve views are shot after every slice and diffed by eye against the last set.
Union Square keys viewpoints to photographs; here they key to the previous build. The URL is
the config surface (`?seed&pos&look&mode&time&tier`), which the boot already canonicalises.

## 10.3 Perf, draw calls first

Higashiyama's `perf.mjs` order: draw calls, triangles, then milliseconds, per viewpoint, on the
saturated fixture. Draw calls are exact and reproducible; wall clock is not. `budget_gate.mjs`
extends to: both projections, all three tiers, and a street viewpoint - the frame is inside its
budget and the estimate is within 25 % of what three drew.

## 10.4 Walkthrough

The gate the street mode cannot ship without. Higashiyama's `walkthrough.mjs` steers the
**player** with the real movement and collision code along legs derived from the corridors,
samples the ground under the feet every few metres, and reports any leg it could not walk and
any discontinuity in the ground. For City Grid the legs come from the same corridor list the
model produces, so adding a road adds a leg. `passability.mjs` sweeps every corridor for a
lane at least 0.68 m plus a margin clear of colliders - the number is the walker's radius
twice, and it is the most consequential constant in Higashiyama's KIT.

## 10.5 Style sheet

`tools/style-sheet.mjs` renders every style from one city, one seed, one camera, side by side.
It gains the tilt and street cameras, so "are these actually different" is answerable for a
facade as well as a roof.

## 10.6 What every gate must record

Failure, not only success (measurement discipline): a capture that could not reach a
viewpoint, a chunk that failed to bake, a car pool that hit its cap, a walkthrough leg that
stalled. And the era: the commit and the tier, because a number from a previous tier is void.
